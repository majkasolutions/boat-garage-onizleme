Boat Garage / Facility & Security
The garage

Indoor, controlled, on camera.

Your boat winters indoors, not out in the open. Humidity and temperature are monitored, the whole area is recorded 24/7 and access is controlled. Even when you are away you can see how your boat is doing.

Facility features

🏠

Indoor space

A concrete indoor garage away from sun, rain and salt air. Boats sit on cradles and trailers with room between them.

🌡️

Humidity & temperature

Ventilation and humidity control prevent mould, odour and corrosion; the interior stays as you left it.

📷

24/7 cameras

The whole area is recorded. If you like, we send you regular photos of your boat on WhatsApp.

🔥

Fire safety

Detection and suppression system, periodic checks of electrical lines, a separate battery charging area.

🛡️

Storage insurance

Your boat is insured for the whole time it stays with us. Policy details come with the contract.

🔧

On-site workshop

Maintenance, repair and polishing under the same roof; the work is done over winter, so you do not wait in spring.

Inside the garage

How it works

Make one call and leave the rest to us. At every step you get a photo update on WhatsApp.

01 · Pick-up

We collect your boat from your marina or our pier and photograph the check-in list.

02 · Haul-out

We lift safely with the travel lift and pressure-wash the hull.

03 · Service

Winterisation, batteries, bilge, system checks and approved repairs.

04 · Garage

Parked in its own bay indoors, humidity controlled. Photos whenever you ask.

05 · Season

Polished and tested, your boat waits for you at the berth.

Let us reserve your bay.
